# Privacy setup checklist

In order. Each business does this once, then reviews it yearly.

## Before the first sale

- [ ] Choose a **Data Protection Officer** (owner or a trusted manager).
- [ ] Fill in **Settings → Privacy contact**: registered name (or trading name
      until registration is done), business address, DPO name, email and
      phone. The public Privacy Notice (`/privacy`) and Terms (`/terms`) fill
      themselves in from this.
- [ ] Open `/privacy` and `/terms` on a phone and read them through once.
- [ ] Sign the **Data Processing Agreement** with Semivra
      ([DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md](DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md)). Fill in
      the hosting regions in section 4.
- [ ] Give every staff member the **Staff Privacy Notice**
      ([STAFF_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md](STAFF_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md)) and keep the signed
      acknowledgement.
- [ ] Give each person their own login, with the narrowest role that works.

## Within the first month

- [ ] Fill in the blanks in the **Privacy Manual**
      ([PRIVACY_MANUAL.md](PRIVACY_MANUAL.md)): retention periods (ask your
      accountant how long tax records must be kept), audit-trail period, DPO.
- [ ] Walk the team through sections 4 and 6 of the manual (everyday rules,
      and what to do if something goes wrong).
- [ ] Check whether **NPC registration** is required (manual, section 7). If it
      is, register the DPO and the processing system at privacy.gov.ph.

## Once DTI / SEC / BIR registration is done

- [ ] Update **Settings → Privacy contact** with the registered name and
      address. The notices update themselves.
- [ ] Re-sign the Data Processing Agreement in the registered name if it was
      first signed under a trading name.

## Every year

- [ ] Review who has access, and remove anyone who no longer needs it.
- [ ] Review the notices, the manual and Semivra's sub-processor list.
- [ ] Re-check the NPC registration thresholds against your numbers.
