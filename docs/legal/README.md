# Privacy and legal documents

Semivra Libellus handles personal information for the businesses that use it:
customers who order, staff who work the till, suppliers, and client accounts.
In the Philippines that brings the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**, its
Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and the National Privacy Commission's
(NPC) circulars into play from the first sale - whether or not the business has
finished its DTI/SEC/BIR registration, and whether or not it has registered
with the NPC.

> **These are drafts, written from what the system actually collects.** They are
> not legal advice. Have them reviewed by a Philippine lawyer or a privacy
> practitioner before relying on them, and again whenever the business or the
> system changes what it collects.

## What is here

| Document | Who it is for | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer Privacy Notice** | Diners, walk-in and QR customers, client-portal accounts, senior citizen and PWD customers | In the app at **`/privacy`**, filled in from Settings → Privacy contact. Linked from the QR menu, the client portal and the login screen. |
| **Terms of Use** | Anyone ordering through the QR menu or the client portal | In the app at **`/terms`** |
| [Staff Privacy Notice](STAFF_PRIVACY_NOTICE.md) | Employees and anyone given a login | Give it to each person when they are added, and keep their signed acknowledgement |
| [Data Processing Agreement](DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md) | Between **Semivra** (the software provider, processing data on the business's behalf) and **each business** using it | Signed once per business, before go-live |
| [Privacy Manual](PRIVACY_MANUAL.md) | The business's owner, managers and DPO | Internal. How data is kept, secured, retained, and what to do when something goes wrong |
| [Setup checklist](CHECKLIST.md) | Whoever sets a business up | Everything to fill in, decide or sign, in order |

## Who is responsible for what

- **The business** (the café, the distributor) decides why and how its
  customers' and staff's information is used. Under the DPA it is the
  **Personal Information Controller (PIC)**. The notices are published in its
  name.
- **Semivra**, which provides and hosts the software, processes that
  information only on the business's instructions. It is a **Personal
  Information Processor (PIP)**. The Data Processing Agreement sets out what it
  may and may not do.

## Filling in the blanks

Anything in `[square brackets]` is for the business to fill in. The in-app
notice and terms fill theirs automatically from **Settings → Privacy contact**;
until those are set, the page shows the trading name and says that the contact
details are being set up.
