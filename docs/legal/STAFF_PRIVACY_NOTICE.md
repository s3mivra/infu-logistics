# Staff Privacy Notice

**[Business registered name]** ("the Business") · [Business address]
Data Protection Officer: [DPO name] · [DPO email] · [DPO phone]
Effective: [date]

This notice explains what personal information the Business keeps about you as
an employee, or as anyone given a login to its point-of-sale and management
system (Semivra Libellus), why it keeps it, and what you can do about it. It is
given to you under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

## 1. What we collect

| Information | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, employee number, role and access permissions | To give you a login and decide what you can see and do | Your employment contract |
| Your password and PIN - **stored only as one-way hashes**; nobody, including the Business and Semivra, can read them | To sign you in and to approve actions that need a manager | Your employment contract |
| When you clock in and out, and your breaks | Time records and payroll | Labor Code record-keeping |
| Your cash shifts: opening float, cash movements, closing count and any variance | Accountability for the cash drawer | Legitimate interest of the Business in protecting its cash |
| What you do in the system: sales, voids, refunds, discounts, stock movements, approvals - with the time and your name | An audit trail, so a mistake or a loss can be traced | Legitimate interest; tax record-keeping |
| The device and browser you signed in from | To manage and end your sessions | Legitimate interest (security) |
| Your pay, commission rate and payslips | Payroll | Your employment contract; Labor Code |
| **Your SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG numbers, and your TIN** | Mandatory contributions and withholding tax | **Required by law** (these are *sensitive personal information* under the DPA) |

We do not collect anything else about you through the system.

## 2. Who can see it

- Your sessions, clock records and the actions you take: managers and the
  owner, according to the permissions they have been given.
- Your government numbers and pay: only people with payroll or accounting
  access.
- **Semivra**, the software provider, stores the system's data on the
  Business's behalf and may see it only to keep the system running or fix a
  fault, under a written Data Processing Agreement.
- Government agencies (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR, DOLE) when the law
  requires the Business to report to them.

Your information is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

## 3. Where it is kept, and for how long

The system's data is held by Semivra's hosting providers: the database on
MongoDB Atlas ([region]) and the application server at Hostinger ([location]).
It is encrypted in transit.

- Payroll and time records: at least **three years** after the last entry
  (Labor Code rules on employment records), and as long as tax law requires
  for anything that supports a tax return.
- Sales and other transaction records that name you as the cashier: as long
  as tax law requires the Business to keep its books ([confirm the period
  with your accountant]).
- Your login is switched off when you leave. Records that must be kept by law
  are kept for the period above, then deleted.

## 4. Your rights

Under the DPA you may:

- **be informed** of how your information is used - this notice;
- **ask for a copy** of the information the Business holds about you;
- **have it corrected** if it is wrong;
- **object** to a use of it, or ask for it to be **deleted or blocked**, where
  the Business has no legal obligation to keep it;
- **ask for it in a portable format**;
- **claim damages** for harm caused by misuse; and
- **complain** to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you
  believe it has been mishandled.

Send any request to the Data Protection Officer above. You will get an answer
within **fifteen (15) working days**.

## 5. Your part

Your password and PIN are yours alone. Do not share them, write them where
others can see, or let anyone ring a sale under your name. Tell a manager at
once if you think someone knows them. Customer information you see at work -
names, numbers, addresses, senior citizen and PWD ID numbers - may be used only
for the order in front of you.
