## SLIDE 5: THE SOLUTION - WHAT IS THE APP?

**Title:** Meet the Semivra L&N App

**WHAT IS IT?**
A unified management software that connects all operations in one digital platform. Instead of using spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual tracking - everything is automated and centralized.

**WHAT IS IT FOR?**
- Manage orders from customer to delivery
- Track inventory (products, drinks, supplies)
- Manage staff and schedules
- See real-time business performance
- Make data-driven decisions

**WHY DO WE NEED IT?**
- Current system is time-consuming and error-prone
- Hard to coordinate between two branches
- Difficult to track performance and profit
- Customers want real-time updates
- Competitors are already doing this

**HOW DOES IT WORK?**
1. Customer places order → Auto-assigned to driver/table
2. Real-time tracking → Customer gets updates
3. Manager sees dashboard → Knows business status instantly
4. Data stored → Can analyze trends and profits
5. Reports generated → Better decision making

**Bottom Line:** One app = Better efficiency + Lower costs + Happy customers
